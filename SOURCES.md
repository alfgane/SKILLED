# Sources and provenance

SKILLED is a fork of
[ethanplusai/astra-flash-orchestrator](https://github.com/ethanplusai/astra-flash-orchestrator)
at commit `bcc7f9eaee051126c0ce821a55194d0b20425b22`, used under the repository's
MIT license.

Native Codex behavior is based on official documentation:

- [Subagents and custom agents](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-configuration/subagents)
- [Codex app-server](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/app-server)

The fork preserves the upstream workflow and installer safety architecture. It
removes DeepSeek, Codex Router, external route/provider configuration, provider
URLs, API-key guidance, and vendor pricing claims.
